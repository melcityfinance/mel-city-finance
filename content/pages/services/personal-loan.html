  • Fixed Rate Loan

    Offers a consistent interest rate and regular repayments throughout the loan term. This type of loan is ideal for those who prefer a set repayment schedule without the worry of changing interest rates

  • Variable Rate Loan

    Interest rates can fluctuate over time, affecting regular repayments. These loans offer flexibility for extra repayments without penalties, allowing you to pay off your loan faster and save on interest.

  • Secured Personal Loan

    You can typically pay a lower rate by using your current car or the car you’re buying as security. This type of loan can make borrowing larger amounts easier, helping you achieve your financial goals with confidence.
