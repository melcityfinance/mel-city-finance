### What Can You Finance with a Business Loan?

Business loans offer a wide range of financing options to support various aspects of your business:

1. **Working Capital**: Maintain smooth daily operations by financing your working capital needs, such as inventory, payroll, and other short-term expenses.
   
2. **Equipment and Machinery**: Invest in new or upgraded equipment and machinery to improve efficiency, productivity, and overall business performance.

3. **New Business or Expansion**: Fund your business expansion plans, whether it’s opening a new location, entering new markets, or increasing production capacity.

4. **Real Estate**: Purchase commercial property for your business operations or invest in real estate to expand your business footprint.

5. **Renovations and Upgrades**: Enhance your business premises with renovations or upgrades to attract more customers and improve operational efficiency.

6. **Marketing and Advertising**: Increase your business visibility and customer base by financing marketing and advertising campaigns.

7. **Debt Consolidation**: Simplify your finances by consolidating existing debts into a single, manageable loan with potentially lower interest rates.
