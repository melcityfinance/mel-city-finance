COMMON QUESTIONS

First home buyer questions

Can I buy a home with less than a 20% deposit?

Potentially. The deposit required depends on the lender, loan structure and your circumstances. Some eligible first home buyers may also have access to government schemes.

Do I need pre-approval before making an offer?

Not always, but understanding your borrowing position before committing to a property can help you make a more informed decision. A pre-approval is conditional and does not guarantee final approval.

What happens if the bank valuation is lower than my purchase price?

A lower valuation can affect the amount a lender is prepared to lend. Depending on the circumstances, you may need additional funds or need to review the proposed loan structure.

Can my parents help with my deposit?

In some circumstances, family assistance such as a gift or other arrangements may be considered. The lender's requirements will depend on how the funds are being provided.

What documents will I need?

The documents depend on your circumstances, but lenders commonly require identification, evidence of income, information about your expenses and liabilities, and evidence of your available funds.

How long does home loan approval take?

Assessment times vary between lenders and can change depending on application complexity, lender processing times and whether further information is required.
