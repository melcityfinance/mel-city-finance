THE PROCESS

What to expect

Every situation is different, but most home loan applications follow a similar path. Here’s what working together typically looks like.

01

Initial Conversation

I start by talking with you about what you're looking to do, where you're currently at and any questions you already have.

02

Understand Your Position

I review your income, expenses, existing commitments and available funds to understand your borrowing position and what may be achievable.

03

Compare Your Options

I research suitable lenders and loan options based on your circumstances, explain the differences and recommend an option for you to consider.

04

Application & Approval

Once you're ready to proceed, I prepare and submit your application and manage it through the lender's assessment process, keeping you updated and helping with any additional requirements along the way.

05

Loan Documents & Settlement

Once the loan is formally approved, I help you understand what happens next and stay involved as the loan documents are completed and your finance moves towards settlement.
