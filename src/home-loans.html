HOME LOANS

Home loan guidance for your next move.

Whether you're buying your next home, investing in property or reviewing your current loan, I can help you understand your borrowing position and the lending options available to you.

Get in Touch How It Works
YOUR NEXT HOME

Buying your next home

Buying again can involve more moving parts than your first purchase, particularly if you already have a home loan or plan to sell your existing property.

Understand Your Borrowing Position

Before you start looking, I can review your income, existing commitments, available equity and expected purchase costs to understand what your next purchase may look like.

Review Your Existing Loan

If you already have a home loan, I can review how it fits with your plans and whether your lending structure may need to change.

Plan Your Next Steps

I can help you understand the finance process and what may need to happen before you make your next purchase.

PROPERTY INVESTMENT

Investing in property

If you're considering an investment property, understanding your borrowing position and lending options early can help you plan your next purchase.

Borrowing Capacity

I can review your income, existing debts, living expenses and current property commitments to understand your borrowing position.

Available Equity

If you already own property, I can help you understand how available equity may fit into the funding of your next purchase.

Lending Options

I can compare suitable lenders and loan options based on your circumstances and explain the differences for you to consider.

HOME LOAN REVIEW

Reviewing your current loan

Your circumstances and the lending market can change over time. A home loan review can help you understand whether your existing loan still suits your needs.

Your Current Loan

I can review your current loan structure, interest rate and features in the context of your present circumstances.

Other Lending Options

Where appropriate, I can compare alternative lending options and explain how they differ from your existing loan.

Costs & Practical Benefit

If refinancing is being considered, I can help you look at the potential costs and practical benefit before deciding whether to proceed.

Thinking about your next move?

Tell me what you're looking to do, and I can help you understand your current position and the lending options available to you.
